Today the tree shows only the 12 links the Quranic Arabic Corpus ontology states.
Below are **extra links where an ayah or a Sahih hadith states the relation itself**.
Nothing is added until the owner approves it (ideally after a scholar checks it).

**Decision (2026-11-15):** the owner approved **A1 and C1–C8**; they are in `data/family.json` → `evidenced`. B1–B5 and C9 are not added.

How each candidate was checked (2026-11-15):

## D. Not proposed (no clear statement in our sources)

- **Sons al-Qasim and Abdullah; daughters Ruqayyah and Umm Kulthum.** Our Bukhari and Muslim data never names them as his children. Bukhari mentions "a daughter of the Messenger of Allah" without a name (e.g. [1342](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:1342)).
- **Al-Hasan and al-Husain as Fatimah's (and Ali's) sons.**
  - He ﷺ calls al-Hasan "this son of mine" ([Bukhari 2704](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:2704)).
  - [Muslim 2404](https://sunnah.com/muslim/44/50) has him call Ali, Fatimah, Hasan and Husain "my family".
  - But no text in our data states who their parents are. They could be added as "called 'my son' / 'my family'" if the owner wants.
- **Hamza as his uncle.** Not stated in our Bukhari/Muslim data.
- **Maryam as "sister of Harun" (19:28).** What "sister" means here differs (Saheeh International adds "[i.e., descendant]"), so it is not a family link.
- **Zakariya and Maryam (3:37).** He was her guardian, not stated as family.
- **Wives.** The tree is parents, children and siblings. Wives could be a later, separate decision.
